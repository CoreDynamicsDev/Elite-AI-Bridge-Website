Privacy notice

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This notice covers the website eliteaibridge.com, run by Core Dynamics Dev. It does not cover the Elite AI Bridge app itself. The app's privacy notes ship with the installer (PRIVACY) and are in the source repository.

The short version

  • No accounts, no forms, no ads, and we never sell data.
  • If you allow it, we use Google Analytics to count visits and clicks on the download and other important links. That is how we know whether the site is useful.
  • You can change your mind any time with .

Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4, a web analytics service from Google. When you allow analytics, Google Analytics records things like:

  • which pages you view, how long you stay, and the site that sent you here;
  • your browser, device type, screen size, language and approximate location (country or city, worked out from your IP address; Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses);
  • clicks on the download button, the GitHub release and source links, the Ko-fi link and the contact email link, plus other links that leave the site. These events record which link was clicked and where on the page, nothing about you.

We only see combined reports. We cannot identify you from them and we do not try to. Google Signals and ad personalization are turned off, and the site sends no data for advertising. Event data is kept for no longer than Google Analytics allows for this site (at most 14 months) and is then deleted.

Google processes this data as our service provider and may process it on servers outside your country, including in the United States. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Cookies and similar technologies

Analytics cookies are only set after you click ALLOW. If you choose NO THANKS, or make no choice, no analytics cookies are set.
